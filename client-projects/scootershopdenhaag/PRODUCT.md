# Product

## Register

brand

## Client

Scootershop Den Haag, Marktweg 384, 2525 JR Den Haag. Owner: Ali. Scooter workshop for
15+ years: repair, maintenance, damage repair, tuning and "keuringen" (snor naar brom,
blauw naar geel kenteken, WOK). Piaggio and Vespa specialist, all brands welcome
(SYM, Peugeot, Gilera, Aprilia, AGM, La Souris, BTC). Open Monday to Saturday
11:00 to 20:00, closed Sunday. Google: 4,5 stars, 309 reviews. WhatsApp and phone
+31 6 34554910, info@scootershopdenhaag.nl. Instagram @scootershop_den_haag.

Current site: WordPress with an Elementor template, stock and AI images, three emoji
cards, broken counters ("1 succesvolle servicebeurten"), "Google Sans" and Roboto.
It buries the two things that actually sell the shop: same-day service and clear
prices.

## Users

Scooter riders in Den Haag and the ring around it (Laak, Schilderswijk, Transvaal,
Rijswijk, Voorburg): students, delivery riders, commuters, 16 to 40. Most arrive on
a phone, often standing next to a scooter that will not start or has a flat. They
Google "scooter reparatie den haag", compare three results in a minute, and decide
on: are they open now, can I bring it today, what does it roughly cost, do people
trust them. A second group is Vespa and Piaggio owners who want a specialist for a
grote beurt or a WOK herkeuring and read a bit further.

## Product Purpose

Turn a search into a WhatsApp message, a call, or a walk-in the same day. The one
promise that no competitor puts first: vandaag gebracht = vandaag klaar. Success is
a tap on WhatsApp or the phone number from the first screen, and a visitor who
knows the price of a grote beurt (€100) before they arrive.

## Brand Personality

Direct. Handy. Haags.

"Jij brengt 'm, wij fixen 'm. Simpel." Speaks like the mechanic at the counter:
informal "je", short sentences, prices out loud, no jargon, no corporate warmth.
Proud of speed and honesty, not of luxury. The logo is a Vespa silhouette in the
Italian tricolour with a heavy rounded wordmark: cheerful, a bit loud, street-level.

## Anti-references

- The current site: Elementor template, three emoji cards, counters that never
  counted, stock mechanic photos with a Meta AI watermark.
- Dealer sites (Vespa.com, Piaggio.nl): glossy lifestyle photography, white space
  as luxury, nothing about price or same-day.
- "Scooter tuning" shops: black backgrounds, red racing stripes, flames, carbon
  texture, aggressive italics.
- Generic local-business templates: purple or blue gradient hero, icons in circles,
  everything centered, "Welkom bij".

## Design Principles

1. **The promise is the hero.** "Vandaag gebracht, vandaag klaar" is the headline,
   and the opening hours and the WhatsApp button sit right next to it. A visitor
   on the sidewalk gets the answer in the first screen.
2. **Prices in the open.** Grote beurt €100, band vanaf €60, accu vanaf €35. Real
   numbers in big type beat "eerlijke prijzen" every time.
3. **Their own workshop, not a stock one.** The Marktweg storefront, the red lift,
   the row of scooters outside. Rough is fine; honest beats pretty.
4. **One page does the job.** Home carries the whole pitch. Subpages exist for
   depth (prijzen, schadeherstel) and for Google, never as the only place a fact
   lives.
5. **Motion explains time.** Anything animated shows the same-day promise (a
   morning-to-evening timeline, an open-now clock), never decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Dutch only, informal "je". Body text 17px minimum on phones; the price
figures and the phone number readable in sunlight. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
Tap targets 44px. No text in images.
