How it runs

Four steps, and you always know where you are.

Every step ends with something you can look at and sign off. Nothing arrives as a surprise at the end.

01

Who has to choose?

We start with the customer, not the design. What are they looking for, what are they still unsure about, and what has to be on screen before they call. That decides the structure of the whole site.

What you get A page plan and a fixed price
02

Design on the real content

No lorem ipsum. We design with your words, prices and photos, because a layout that looks good with placeholder content falls apart the moment the real text goes in.

What you get The design, plus two rounds of feedback
03

Built, not assembled

Hand-written code instead of a stack of plugins. That makes the site faster, safer and cheaper to maintain, and there is no theme licence to renew every year.

What you get A preview link to check it on your own phone
04

Live, in your name

Domain, code, content and accounts are in your name, not ours. You can walk away at any point and take everything with you. That is not a favour, it is how it should work.

What you get Every login, and a short handover on running it
