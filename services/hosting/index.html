What is in it

Six things that quietly go wrong.

A website is not a piece of furniture. Certificates expire, hosts get slow, backups turn out to be empty. These are the things nobody thinks about until they go wrong.

SSL and security

The padlock in the address bar stays valid, renewed automatically. Let it expire and every visitor sees a red warning instead of your site.

Daily backups

A copy every day, kept for thirty days, and tested to make sure it actually restores. A backup you have never tried is not a backup.

Speed watched

Sites get slow by drift, usually one oversized photo somebody added in a hurry. We measure it and fix it before it starts costing you visitors.

Uptime checks

The site is checked every few minutes. If it goes down we know before you hear it from a customer who could not place an order.

Updates and small changes

New opening hours, a price that changed, a member of staff added. You email it, we make it. No editing system you would never use, and no quote for one line of text.

One person to call

No ticket system and no account manager. You email the person who built your site, and they already know what you are talking about.

What we do

What actually happens in a month.

A monthly fee with nothing visible against it feels like money thrown away. So here is what happens, including in a month when you hear nothing from us.

Every day

A full backup is taken and stored somewhere other than the site itself. A backup next to the server is not a backup.

Every few minutes

The site is requested from the outside. If it does not answer, we get the alert, rather than you getting the phone call from a customer.

Every month

Updates are reviewed and applied, and the SSL certificate checked. On Plus and Growth you get a short report: visitors, enquiries, and anything that stood out.

Every quarter

Load time is measured again and compared against launch. If the site has slowed down, we find out why and fix it. That costs you nothing extra.

Every year

The domain is renewed, in your name. You are told before it happens, not after.

Minimum term 6 or 12 mo

Basic runs for a minimum of twelve months, Plus and Growth for six. After that one email is enough to stop: no notice period and no penalty.

Ownership Yours

The domain is registered with you as the holder from day one, not us. That is the difference between moving house and starting over.

If you leave All of it

You get the code, the files and every login. We help you move to your new provider, even when that is a competitor.
