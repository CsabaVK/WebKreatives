The fine print

What the number does and does not cover.

Nothing sours a project like a price that grows afterwards. So here is what sits outside it, up front.

Included

  • Custom design, no template
  • Build, testing and going live
  • Works on phone, tablet and desktop
  • General SEO and speed, included as standard
  • Analytics, so you can see what happens
  • Two rounds of feedback before launch
  • Changes after launch go through us, via your Hosting plan
  • Domain, code and accounts in your name

Not included

  • VAT
  • Contact form, unless ticked above
  • Domain and hosting, see the support plan
  • Copy and photography, unless ticked above
  • Paid ads and ad budget
  • Third-party licences, such as a booking system
  • Unusual migrations off a legacy platform
