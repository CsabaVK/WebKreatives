08 · Carousel arc

Five slides, one arc.

Hook, pain, steps, proof, join. Grab attention, name the pain, show the system, prove it works, drive the action. One hero word on the cover; a list of what breaks; the mechanism as a numbered list; outcomes as checkmarks; a red closing slide with the URL. Rendered as a PDF for LinkedIn documents by node _design/carousel.cjs spec.json out/.

01 · HookCover
Outgrown templates 01 / 05

Outgrown.

The template did its job. Now it costs you clients. What a custom site changes, in four slides.

Swipe

One hero word at 236px, accent on one syllable or none. Subline: the pain and the payoff in two sentences. Swipe cue bottom-right.

02 · PainProblem
The problem 02 / 05

What breaks first.

  • The site looks like every competitor's
  • Every change waits on a plugin update
  • Google cannot tell what you do
  • The phone number is three taps away
  • You do not own the thing you paid for
Swipe

Paper ground. Name the problem in the headline, then three to five things that break, each one line, red cross markers.

03 · StepsSystem
How a build runs 03 / 05

The mechanism.

  1. A short brief: what the business does and what the site is not doing
  2. A fixed price back within one business day
  3. Design and build by the same person, feedback rounds included
  4. Launch, then hosting and support on one monthly plan
Swipe

Ink ground. The mechanism that solves it as a numbered list, three or four steps, one line each, red numerals.

04 · ProofResult
What you get 04 / 05

What you walk away with.

  • A site built around how your buyers decide
  • Domain, code and accounts in your name
  • Found on Google for what you actually do
  • Someone to call when something breaks
Swipe

Outcomes as checkmarks. A specific number goes here once the owner clears numbers; until then a checkable outcome per line. One mark allowed.

05 · JoinCTA
Start 05 / 05

Send the problem, not the solution.

A short brief is enough. You get a fixed price back, or the honest answer that it should be scoped differently.

webkreatives.com

The only red ground in the system. White type, ink accent word, the URL set in display. No comment keyword, no price line until cleared.

09 · Single post arc

One image, one stage.

A LinkedIn image post carries one image, so the five stages split: the image carries one stage, the post text carries the other four. The image stops the scroll; the text does the reasoning. Hook on the image by default, proof when a screenshot or a list beats a sentence, pain on paper at most one in four, join never. Worked example with the render command and the full post text in _design/single-example.md.
