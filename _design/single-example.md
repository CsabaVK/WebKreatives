# Single post, worked example

One LinkedIn image post following the single post arc (S) in DESIGN.md, section 7.
The image carries the hook. The post text carries pain, mechanism, proof and join.

## Image

T1 Statement, portrait, two-line headline, one accent word, lede on, no numbers.

```
node _design/render.cjs statement out/single.png --eyebrow "Ownership" --headline "Own what you *paid* for." --lede "Domain, code and accounts in your name, not ours."
```

Check `out/single.png` at 40% width before it goes anywhere. The headline has to read at
that size; the lede may not.

## Alt text

```
Own what you paid for. WebKreatives.
```

## Post text

Hook, pain, mechanism, proof, join. 121 words. The hook lands in 102 characters, before
the fold on a phone. The lede is not repeated.

```
Most business websites are rented.
The domain, the code, the logins: someone else's name on all of it.

Then the builder stops answering. Or the monthly plan doubles. Or you want to move, and learn that the site cannot come with you.

The domain is registered to you before we write a line.
The code sits in a repository in your name; we work inside it.
Hosting and every account open on your email, with passwords you can change the same day.

Cancel our hosting tomorrow and the site keeps running, because it was never ours to switch off.

Send us the site you are renting. You get back what moving it into your name costs, as a fixed price. webkreatives.com
```
