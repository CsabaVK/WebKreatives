4Ownership

  • The domain is and stays registered in the client's name. WebKreatives is granted access as a user and never becomes the registrant.
  • On payment of the build price, all files of the website belong to the client. The client can request a full copy at any time.
  • Accounts opened for the site (hosting, Google, analytics) are opened in the client's name where the provider allows it; WebKreatives is added as a user or manager.
  • The client keeps ownership of all content they supply and confirms they hold the rights to use it. WebKreatives may show the finished site in its portfolio unless the client objects in writing.

Passwords are never shared. If the client is silent for 30 calendar days the project may be paused; after 60 days it may be ended, on the basis set out in Annex A.

6Availability and support

WebKreatives keeps the site online with best-effort availability, monitors it, and restores it from backup where needed. Requests are answered within one business day. Small text and image changes are made within a business day on every plan; larger work follows the plan's allowance.

7Ending the agreement

The build part of this agreement ends when the site is live and the build invoice is paid. The hosting plan runs for its minimum term and then month to month, ending one month after either party gives notice by email. On ending, WebKreatives hands over all files, removes itself from the client's accounts, and the client's domain and email are unaffected. Either party may end the agreement immediately if the other seriously breaches it and does not put it right within 14 days of a written request.

8Liability, law and the general terms

Liability is limited as set out in Annex A. Dutch law applies; disputes go to the competent court in Amsterdam. The general terms in Annex A apply; where this agreement and Annex A differ, this agreement prevails. Changes to this agreement are made in writing. A scanned or electronic signature has the same effect as a handwritten one.
